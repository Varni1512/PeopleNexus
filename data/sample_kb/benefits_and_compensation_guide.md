# Employee Benefits, Rewards & Compensation Guide - PeoplePrime Technologies

This guide details the specific financial, wellness, medical, and development benefits available to active full-time employees.

---

## 1. Health & Wellness Allowances

### 1.1 Annual Health Checkup
- Every permanent employee is eligible for an annual comprehensive executive health checkup package valued up to **₹4,500 ($60 USD)** at accredited diagnostic centers (Apollo, Dr. Lal PathLabs, Quest Diagnostics).
- Appointments are booked directly via the *CareWellness 360* app with cashless vouchers.

### 1.2 Gym, Fitness & Sports Reimbursement
- To encourage physical health, the company reimburses up to **₹1,200 per month (or $20 USD)** towards gym memberships, yoga classes, swimming sessions, or fitness app subscriptions (Cult.fit, Strava, Apple Fitness+).
- Invoices must be submitted quarterly under the Health Reimbursement tab in the HRMS portal.

---

## 2. Learning & Professional Development (L&D)

### 2.1 Annual Upskilling & Certification Budget
- Each confirmed employee is allocated an annual personal learning stipend of **₹40,000 (or $500 USD)** per financial year.
- Approved expenses include AWS/GCP/Azure certifications, Coursera, Udemy, specialized technical bootcamps, and relevant industry conferences.
- Prior approval from the reporting manager is required before course registration. Reimbursement is processed upon submission of the passing completion certificate.

### 2.2 Higher Education Sponsorship
- Employees with over 2 years of continuous tenure may apply for corporate tuition reimbursement for accredited master's degrees (MBA, MS in Computer Science/Data Science) with up to 50% tuition sponsorship (subject to a 24-month service agreement).

---

## 3. Employee Recognition, Referral Bonus & Rewards

### 3.1 Talent Referral Bonus Program
- Employees who refer qualified candidates hired for full-time regular roles receive referral rewards paid 90 days after the candidate's joining date:
  - Junior / Associate Roles: **₹25,000 ($300 USD)**
  - Mid-Level / Senior Engineers (L3/L4): **₹50,000 ($600 USD)**
  - Lead / Principal / Engineering Managers (L5+): **₹1,00,000 ($1,200 USD)**
- Referrals must be submitted through the internal Greenhouse careers portal prior to the candidate's first interview.

### 3.2 Spot Awards & Quarterly Excellence Rewards
- **Kudos Spot Award:** Instant recognition of ₹5,000 gift voucher given by managers for exceptional week-on-week customer impact.
- **Quarterly Star Performer Award:** Trophy and ₹25,000 cash bonus awarded across departments during all-hands meetings.

---

## 4. Retiral Benefits: Provident Fund, Gratuity & NPS

### 4.1 Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)
- Mandatory 12% contribution of basic pay by the employee, matched by 12% employer contribution (split between EPF and EPS).
- Universal Account Number (UAN) transfers must be initiated via the EPFO unified portal within 30 days of joining.

### 4.2 National Pension Scheme (NPS) Corporate Model
- Employees may opt for corporate NPS tax deductions under Section 80CCD(2), deducting up to 10% of basic pay directly through monthly payroll for long-term retirement savings.

### 4.3 Statutory Gratuity
- Payable upon retirement, resignation, or termination to employees who have rendered at least **5 years of continuous service**.
- Calculated in accordance with the Payment of Gratuity Act: `(15 * Last Drawn Basic Salary * Years of Service) / 26`.
