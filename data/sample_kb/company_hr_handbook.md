# PeoplePrime Technologies & NovaRetail Enterprise HR Policy Handbook

Welcome to the official Enterprise HR Policy Handbook. This comprehensive document applies to all full-time, part-time, and contractual employees across all business entities, technology centers, and retail locations.

---

## 1. Working Hours, Attendance & Hybrid Work Policy

### 1.1 Standard Working Hours & Core Collaboration Hours
- Standard operational working hours are **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM**, Monday through Friday (40 hours per week).
- Each employee is entitled to a **60-minute lunch break** and two discretionary 15-minute wellness breaks.
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** All teams must be available and responsive on Slack and email between **11:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time** for synchronized team meetings and cross-functional coordination.

### 1.2 Hybrid & Remote Work (Work-From-Home) Policy
- Eligible office-based and corporate technology employees may work remotely **up to 2 days per week** (e.g., Tuesday & Thursday or Monday & Friday) with prior reporting manager approval.
- Retail store staff, warehouse personnel, and frontline field engineers are non-remote operational roles and follow standard store shift rotas.
- **Work-From-Home Allowances:**
  - **One-time Ergonomic Setup Reimbursement:** Up to **₹25,000 (or $300 USD)** for home office setup (desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, or keyboard) upon completion of probation.
  - **Monthly High-Speed Internet Stipend:** Reimbursable up to **₹1,500 (or $25 USD) per month** against active broadband invoices submitted via the HRMS portal.

## 2. Leave and Absence Management

### 2.1 Annual Paid Leave (Privilege Leave)
- Full-time permanent employees accrue **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year (accrued at 1.66 days per completed month of service).
- Leave requests must be applied through the HRMS portal at least **5 working days in advance** for leaves exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- **Carry-Forward Policy:** Up to **5 unused annual leaves** may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Carried-over leaves must be utilized on or before **March 31**, failing which they automatically lapse. Leave encashment is only processed at the time of final separation/offboarding.

### 2.2 Casual & Sick Leave
- **Casual Leave (CL):** 8 days per calendar year for unforeseen personal emergencies. Cannot be combined with Annual Leave.
- **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 paid days per calendar year for personal illness or medical recovery.
- For medical absences exceeding **2 consecutive business days**, a registered medical practitioner's certificate/prescription must be uploaded to HRMS upon resumption of work.

### 2.3 Parental & Maternity Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** Female employees who have completed at least 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months are entitled to **26 weeks (182 calendar days) of fully paid maternity leave** for up to the first two surviving children (in accordance with the Maternity Benefit Act).
- **Paternity Leave:** Eligible male employees receive **10 continuous working days of fully paid paternity leave**, usable within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
- **Adoption Leave:** 12 weeks of paid leave for primary adoptive parents of an infant under 1 year of age.

### 2.4 Bereavement & Compassionate Leave
- Employees receive up to **5 consecutive paid working days** of bereavement leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law). Additional unpaid compassionate leave can be approved by the Department Head.

## 3. Compensation, Payroll & Tax Management

### 3.1 Payroll Cycle & Cut-Off Dates
- Monthly salary is processed and credited on the **final business day of each calendar month**.
- The monthly payroll attendance and deduction cut-off date is the **22nd of each month**. Any overtime, reimbursements, or leave balance adjustments approved after the 22nd will be settled in the following month's payroll cycle.

### 3.2 Reimbursements & Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP)
- Eligible tax-exempt reimbursements (Fuel, Driver Allowance, Telephone/Broadband, LTA, Books & Periodicals) must be claimed via HRMS by the **20th of each month** accompanied by valid GST invoices.
- **Form 16 & Tax Declarations:** Investment declarations open on April 15th, and final proof submission closes on January 20th. Form 16 Part A and Part B are issued via the employee tax vault before June 15th annually.

### 3.3 Annual Performance Appraisals & Bonuses
- The annual performance review cycle runs from **January to February**, with ratings calibrated on a 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exceeds Expectations) scale.
- Salary revisions, merit increments, and annual bonus payouts are communicated in **March** and take effect from **April 1st** of each financial year.

## 4. Health, Medical & Wellness Benefits

### 4.1 Group Medical Insurance (GMC)
- All confirmed full-time employees and their dependents (Spouse + up to 2 dependent children up to age 25) are enrolled under the corporate Group Health Insurance policy with a comprehensive coverage sum insured of **₹5,00,000 (or $10,000 USD)** per family floater.
- **Pre-existing Diseases & Maternity Cover:** Covered from Day 1 without waiting periods. Maternity hospitalization expenses covered up to ₹75,000 for normal delivery and ₹1,00,000 for C-section.
- **Parental Health Insurance Add-on:** Employees can opt to cover dependent parents or parents-in-law with a co-pay contribution deducted via monthly payroll.

### 4.2 Group Term Life & Personal Accident Insurance
- **Term Life Insurance:** Provides coverage equivalent to **3 times the employee's annual Fixed CTC** in the event of death during active employment.
- **Group Personal Accident (GPA):** Covers accidental disability and critical trauma hospitalization up to 2 times the annual CTC.

### 4.3 Employee Assistance Program (EAP) & Mental Wellbeing
- Free, confidential, 24/7 mental wellness counseling, therapy sessions, and financial advisory are available to all employees and their immediate families through our partner provider *CareWellness 360* (Toll-Free Helpline: `1800-419-CARE` or email `support@carewellness360.com`).

## 5. Travel & Business Expense Policy

### 5.1 Travel Class Entitlements
- **Air Travel:** Economy class on commercial airlines for domestic and international flights under 6 hours duration. Business class allowed only for Senior Directors and above, or flights exceeding 8 continuous hours with prior VP approval.
- **Rail Travel:** AC 2-Tier or Executive Chair Car for travel under 6 hours.

### 5.2 Daily Per-Diem Meal & Lodging Allowances
- **Tier 1 Cities (Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore, New York, London):** Hotel accommodation reimbursement up to **₹5,500/night** (domestic) or **$180/night** (international). Daily meal per-diem: **₹2,000/day** (domestic) or **$60/day** (international).
- **Tier 2/3 Cities:** Hotel reimbursement up to **₹3,500/night**; Daily meal per-diem: **₹1,200/day**.
- Corporate Cab / Local Transit: Reimbursements provided for airport transfers and client visits via Uber for Business or Ola Corporate profiles.

### 5.3 Expense Reporting Timeline
- All travel and entertainment expense claims must be filed within **15 calendar days** of completing travel. Expenses submitted beyond 30 days require exceptional approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## 6. IT Equipment, Data Security & Confidentiality

### 6.1 Hardware Allocation & Laptop Policy
- Standard issue hardware: Apple MacBook Pro (14-inch, M-series) for Engineering and Product; Dell Latitude / Lenovo ThinkPad (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) for Operations, HR, and Sales.
- All company assets remain company property and must be maintained in good working order. Hardware refresh occurs every **36 months** of continuous service.
- **Lost or Stolen Assets:** Must be reported to the IT Helpdesk (`it-security@peopleprime.com`) within **2 hours** to initiate remote data wipe and security lockdown.

### 6.2 Data Protection & Acceptable Use Policy
- Company VPN must be actively enabled whenever accessing internal corporate databases, customer PII, code repositories, or staging environments.
- Storing confidential company data on personal unencrypted USB drives or public cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox) is strictly prohibited and constitutes gross misconduct.
- Use of unauthorized AI tools with proprietary source code or confidential HR documents is prohibited under our Corporate AI Governance Guidelines.

## 7. Workplace Conduct, Harassment Prevention (POSH) & Ethics

### 7.1 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
- PeoplePrime Technologies upholds a strict **Zero-Tolerance Policy** against sexual harassment, hostile workplace environments, stalking, and gender discrimination.
- An **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** is formally constituted under the POSH Act 2013, presided over by a senior woman presiding officer and an external independent NGO representative.
- Any aggrieved employee may file a written complaint to `posh-committee@peopleprime.com`. All proceedings are strictly confidential and concluded within 90 days. Retaliation against complainants or witnesses is punishable by immediate termination.

### 7.2 Whistleblower Protection & Ethics Helpline
- Employees may report financial irregularities, fraud, insider trading, bribery, or ethics violations anonymously via the 24/7 Whistleblower Portal or direct email to `ethics-hotline@peopleprime.com`.

## 8. Performance Management, Promotions & PIP

### 8.1 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- When an employee consistently falls below role expectations (Rating 1 or 2 during periodic reviews), a structured **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** may be initiated by the manager in consultation with HR.
- **PIP Duration:** Standard duration is **30 to 60 calendar days** with clearly defined, objective weekly milestones and written bi-weekly reviews.
- Successful completion restores good standing; failure to meet the agreed milestones results in separation due to performance insufficiency.

### 8.2 Promotion Cycles
- Promotions are evaluated twice a year during the **April and October** talent review windows. Key criteria include sustained high performance (Rating 4 or 5 for minimum 2 consecutive cycles), leadership impact, and readiness for next-level responsibilities.

## 9. Probation, Resignation, Notice Period & Offboarding

### 9.1 Probation Period
- All newly hired regular employees undergo a **6-month probationary period**.
- Performance is formally evaluated in the 5th month. Upon successful review, a written confirmation letter is issued. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months if performance metrics require further evaluation.

### 9.2 Resignation & Notice Period Policy
- **During Probation:** Notice period is **30 calendar days** from either the employee or the company.
- **Confirmed Full-Time Employees:** Notice period is **60 calendar days (2 months)** for individual contributors, and **90 calendar days (3 months)** for Senior Managers, Directors, and Executive Leadership.
- **Notice Period Buyout:** Shortening notice period via buyout is subject to operational business feasibility and requires written approval from the Department Vice President and Head of HR.

### 9.3 Offboarding & Full & Final (F&F) Settlement
- The employee must complete the digital handover checklist, transfer all credentials/knowledge bases, and physically return all assigned company assets (laptop, charger, access cards, monitor) by the final working day.
- **Full & Final Settlement Timeline:** The final salary, accrued leave encashment, gratuity calculation (if applicable for tenure > 5 years), and official relieving/experience letters are finalized and disbursed within **30 to 45 calendar days** of the last working day.
